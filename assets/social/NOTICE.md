# Social logos (Übersicht "Mitmachen")

Brand marks used only to name the project's own channels (GitHub, YouTube, X).

| File | Source | License / basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `github-mark.svg` | Path from simple-icons 14.13.0 (`icons/github.svg`), taken from the GitHub mark | CC0 (simple-icons); the mark itself is GitHub's, used in black or white per [GitHub's logo guidelines](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo) |
| `x-logo.svg` | Path from simple-icons 14.13.0 (`icons/x.svg`) | CC0 (simple-icons); the mark is X's, used in black or white |
| `youtube-logo.svg` | Frame and play mark from simple-icons 14.13.0 (`icons/youtube.svg`), frame in YouTube red `#FF0000`, play mark in white | CC0 (simple-icons); the colors follow YouTube's brand resources |

The marks are shown unaltered in shape, with the standard black/white (GitHub, X) or brand red (YouTube) colors; no
other colors, effects or shadows. The app tints the black/white marks to the theme's text color.
