# Benchmark reference clip

`reference-de.wav` (16 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM, about 6 s) is the German sentence
the speed measurement transcribes: "Kurzer Test: Ich diktiere diesen Satz,
damit WhisPaste misst, wie schnell dein Rechner Sprache in Text verwandelt."

Synthesized with the Piper voice `de_DE-thorsten-medium` (trained on the
Thorsten-Voice dataset, CC0) through sherpa-onnx (Apache-2.0). The clip itself
is released under CC0 like its source voice data.
