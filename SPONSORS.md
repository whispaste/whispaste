# Sponsors

WhisPaste is free and open source, funded by voluntary sponsoring (GitHub Sponsors, Ko-fi)
that covers real recurring fixed costs (Apple Developer Program, Microsoft Partner Center,
hosting/domain).

This file recognizes sponsors who opt in to being named here. Listing is **opt-in only** —
sponsoring WhisPaste never adds you here automatically, and no sponsor list is maintained
outside of what people explicitly ask to be added to. If you sponsor the project and would
like to be listed, open a discussion or reach out via the contact details in the repo — a
name, a link, and (optionally) a short note is all that's needed.
