Speech-to-text for your desktop: WhisPaste turns your voice into text and pastes it where your cursor is — in any app. Press a hotkey, speak, done. No copy-paste, no app switching.

Works everywhere: Outlook, Teams, VS Code, Chrome, Slack, or any other window.

🔒 YOUR AUDIO STAYS LOCAL
Three Whisper models (Small, Medium, and Large v3 Turbo) plus the extra-fast, CPU-only Parakeet engine transcribe your speech entirely on your machine — your audio never leaves your device. Up to 99 languages with Whisper. Cloud options (OpenAI, Deepgram) are available but always opt-in, and audio goes directly to the provider, never through WhisPaste servers. No account required.

💶 ONE-TIME, NOT A SUBSCRIPTION
No subscription, no time limits, no recurring costs. WhisPaste is open source (MIT license) and built transparently on GitHub.

📋 HISTORY & EXPORT
Every recording is saved automatically. Search, tag, pin, archive, and export to TXT, Markdown, JSON, CSV, or Word.

⌨️ VOICE SHORTCUTS
Say a short trigger — WhisPaste expands it into the full text before pasting. Perfect for signatures, greetings, and frequently typed phrases.

🎛️ BUILT FOR DAILY WORK
Push-to-talk or toggle · Floating Button above your desktop · System tray integration · Auto-start with Windows · English, German, and Hebrew UI
