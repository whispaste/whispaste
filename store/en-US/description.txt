WhisPaste turns your voice into text right where your cursor is, in any Windows app. Press a hotkey, speak, done. No copy and paste, no switching windows.

It works wherever you type: Outlook, Teams, Word, VS Code, Chrome, Slack and every other app.

🔒 YOUR VOICE STAYS ON YOUR PC
Whisper (three model sizes, 99 languages) and the fast Parakeet engine (about 25 European languages, CPU only) transcribe entirely on your computer. Recordings are not kept after transcription. A graphics card speeds Whisper up through Vulkan but is never required. After the one-time model download, transcription works without an internet connection. Cloud transcription with OpenAI or Deepgram is optional and uses your own API key; the audio goes straight to the provider, never through a WhisPaste server. No account needed.

✨ SMART MODE (OPTIONAL)
Clean up filler words and punctuation, shorten a text or translate it into seven languages before it is pasted. Runs on a local AI model or through OpenAI with your own key. Select text in any app and say what should change, and WhisPaste rewrites it in place.

📋 HISTORY, NOTES & EXPORT
Every transcript is saved locally and searchable. Tag, pin, archive, merge and export to TXT, Markdown, JSON, CSV or Word. A PIN lock keeps the history private. Notes and quick notes keep longer thoughts in one place.

⌨️ VOICE SNIPPETS & YOUR OWN WORDS
Say a trigger word and WhisPaste inserts the full text: signatures, greetings, phrases you type every day. Interactive snippets fill templates field by field. Custom vocabulary and replacements make names and jargon come out right.

🎛️ BUILT FOR DAILY WORK
Toggle or push-to-talk · Live overlay with optional live preview · Floating button · Clipboard side panel at the screen edge · System tray and autostart · Interface in English, German, Hebrew, Russian, Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil)
