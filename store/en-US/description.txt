WhisPaste is desktop voice input: turn your voice into text and paste it where your cursor is — in any app. Press a hotkey, speak, done. Speech to text with no copy-paste and no app switching.

Voice to text that lands right in the field: Outlook, Teams, VS Code, Chrome, Slack, or any other window. On-device speech recognition instead of a cloud requirement.

🔒 YOUR AUDIO STAYS LOCAL
Three Whisper AI models (Small, Medium, and Large v3 Turbo) run entirely on your machine — your speech recognition never leaves your device. Cloud options (OpenAI, Deepgram) are available but always opt-in, and audio goes directly to the provider, never through WhisPaste servers. No account required.

📋 HISTORY & EXPORT
Every transcription is saved automatically. Search, pin, archive, and export to TXT, Markdown, JSON, CSV, or Word.

⌨️ VOICE SHORTCUTS
Say a short trigger — WhisPaste expands it into the full text before pasting. Perfect for signatures, greetings, and frequently typed phrases.

🎛️ BUILT FOR DAILY WORK
Push-to-talk or toggle · Floating Button above your desktop · System tray integration · Auto-start with Windows · English, German, and Hebrew UI
