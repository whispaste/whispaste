API Costs

Useful if you use cloud transcription and want a realistic sense of what typical usage costs over time.

Whisper is billed per audio minute (~$0.006/min). A short sentence costs fractions of a cent; typical monthly usage is $1–5.

Occasional (~5 min/day) ~$1/mo
Regular (~20 min/day) ~$4/mo
Heavy (~60 min/day) ~$11/mo
