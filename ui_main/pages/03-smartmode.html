Automatically pick a preset based on which app you're using when recording starts

How it works
1 You record and transcribe as usual
2 The transcription is refined by AI using your chosen preset
3 The improved text is pasted — no extra steps needed
App detection is active — the preset is automatically selected based on the focused application. Manual selection is disabled.

Everyday

Cleanup Fixes grammar, punctuation, and filler words — keeps your original meaning intact so um yeah I think we should like meet tomorrow → I think we should meet tomorrow.
Concise Removes verbosity and redundancy while keeping the core message and tone Due to the fact that it is raining → Because it's raining
Email Reformats your speech into a professional email with greeting and sign-off tell Sarah the report is done → Hi Sarah, the report is complete…
Bullet Points Extracts key points from your speech and organizes them as a clear list
Translate Translates your transcription into the target language you choose below
