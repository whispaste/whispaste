Speech-to-text for your Mac: press a hotkey, speak, and WhisPaste writes your words right where your cursor is, in any app: Mail, Notes, Slack, VS Code or anywhere else you type.

YOUR AUDIO STAYS ON YOUR MAC
Two on-device engines do the work: Whisper (Small, Medium or Large v3 Turbo, up to 99 languages, accelerated by Metal on Apple Silicon) and the especially fast Parakeet. After a one-time model download, transcription runs offline and your audio never leaves your Mac. Cloud transcription via OpenAI or Deepgram is optional, uses your own API key and goes straight to the provider, never through WhisPaste servers. No account required.

SMART MODE
Have your text cleaned up, shortened or translated before it is pasted, on your Mac or via OpenAI. You can also apply it to a past transcription later.

EDIT SELECTED TEXT BY VOICE
Select a sentence in any app, press the selection hotkey and say what should change, such as "make it friendlier". WhisPaste rewrites the selection in place. Off by default.

VOICE SNIPPETS
Say a short trigger and WhisPaste inserts the full text: signatures, greetings, recurring phrases, or interactive templates with several fields.

HISTORY & EXPORT
Every transcription is saved on your Mac. Search, tag, pin, archive and merge entries, protect the history with a PIN, and export to TXT, Markdown, JSON, CSV or Word.

ONE-TIME PURCHASE, NOT A SUBSCRIPTION
No subscription, no time limits, no recurring costs. WhisPaste is open source (MIT license) and developed in the open on GitHub. Your purchase directly supports its development.
